# Octagon Tennis Court reservation policy

- Reservations can ONLY be requested two days in advance.   
    - e.g., Reservation request in Aug 3rd can be issued between Aug 1st to Aug 3rd.
- Reservations can be submitted Monday through Friday between 8AM and 4PM.
- Requests submitted outside of these times will be canceled.
- Monday/Tuesday and Holiday reservations may be submitted on Fridays.
- Same day reservations must be submitted no later than 3PM on weekdays ONLY.
- First reservation slot begins at 7:00AM; last reservation slot starts at 9:00PM.
- One-hour reservations per day only.
- Successful submission of reservation request does not guarantee reservation. Upon successful request, an email titled "Pending Approval" is sent to the issuer's email address. Until a reservation for a time slot is decided, multiple requests from different emails can be waiting for approval for the same time slot. Then RIOC (Roosevelt Island Operation Corporation) admin manually approves one request per time slot, probably the one that's issued at the earliest time. Upon approval, an email titled "Your Tennis Permit has been approved" is sent to the issuer's email address.
    - When a reservation request is disapproved, an email titled "Your permit request has been canceled" or "Unable to Process your request" is sent to the email.
